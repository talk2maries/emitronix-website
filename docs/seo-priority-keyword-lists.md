## Directional-data caveat and evidence guardrails

- Search volume, keyword difficulty (KD) and CPC are **directional UAE planning bands**, not live measurements. No Keyword Planner, Search Console, Ahrefs or Semrush export was supplied. Validate shortlisted terms in a dated first-party or paid dataset before setting traffic, lead or budget forecasts.
- CPC is shown in AED. KD is a modeled 0–100 range. Low-volume location terms can still have high commercial value.
- Verified services are civil contracting, main contracting, warehouse construction, industrial buildings, commercial buildings, villa construction, interior fit-out, building renovation, structural works, design and build, turnkey construction, project management and authority-approval coordination.
- MEP is verified only as interface, inspection-readiness and documentation coordination. Standalone MEP contracting is not verified.
- Structural testing checkpoints and handover readiness are published; standalone testing and commissioning is not verified.
- DIP, JAFZA, Dubai South, Jebel Ali, Al Quoz and Business Bay appear in the repository. Dubai Industrial City, DMCC/JLT and DSO are research opportunities marked `Hold` until company coverage and relevant scope are verified.
- “Owner” means the canonical page or proposed content asset that should own the query. It does not assert that a proposed page already exists.
- Do not publish claims of authority endorsement, guaranteed approval, local offices, completed projects, client names, certifications or results without evidence and publication consent.
