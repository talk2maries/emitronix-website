## Non-negotiable source and disclosure rules

- The 38 JPEG files in the desktop `Photos` directory are private visual references only.
- No reference JPEG may be copied into `public`, uploaded, published, embedded, passed to an image-generation model, or used as an image-editing target.
- Generation is text-only. The group labels below communicate only broad subject matter and professional mood.
- Every output must be a newly generated, clearly original representative scene. It must not recreate any identifiable person, project, building, sign, logo, document, vehicle, registration plate, or confidential site detail from a reference file.
- Generated people are synthetic and representative. They are not Emitronix employees.
- Generated construction and approval scenes are illustrative. They are not evidence of completed Emitronix work, authority approval, authority endorsement, or a real Emitronix project.
- No authority, developer, client, consultant, contractor, equipment-maker, or vehicle brand marks are permitted.
- Homepage branding exception approved on 27 July 2026: assets 01-03 may show only the exact, repository-owned Emitronix logo on the backs of representative PPE. This exception does not permit reference-photo branding, third-party marks, fake authority branding, or any claim that the scene depicts a real Emitronix project or employee.
- No embedded text, document text, project boards, watermarks, or fake safety/approval signage are permitted.
- The existing premium white-and-blue visual system, routes, forms, analytics, cookie consent, and Arabic functionality remain unchanged.
