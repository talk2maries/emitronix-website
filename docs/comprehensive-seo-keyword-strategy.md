## Executive decision summary

|---|---|---|---|
| Protect one query owner per intent | Homepage owns `construction company Dubai`; `/main-contracting` owns `main contractor Dubai`; `/civil` owns `civil contractor Dubai`. | These pages currently use related language and can dilute one another if titles, H1s and anchors become interchangeable. | Critical |
| Prioritize five commercial pages | Focus first on civil, warehouse, villa, interior fit-out and main contracting. | These combine verified service relevance, direct buyer intent and meaningful contract value. | Critical |
| Use the supplied on-page brief as the baseline | Preserve its target recommendations for warehouse, DM, civil, DEWA, DDA, villa, Trakhees, turnkey and interior pages, subject to the intent refinements in this report. | It correctly separates several high-value service and approval intents. | Critical |

## Metric methodology

No dated export from Google Ads Keyword Planner, Google Search Console, Ahrefs or Semrush was supplied. Search volume, keyword difficulty and CPC are therefore **directional UAE planning bands**, not measured forecasts.

| Metric | Bands used | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| UAE monthly search volume | `<10`, `10–20`, `20–50`, `50–100`, `100–250`, `250–500`, `500–1,000` | Modeled English-language UAE demand. A cluster of low-volume local phrases can still generate valuable enquiries. |
| Difficulty, KD 0–100 | `5–15`, `10–25`, `15–30`, `20–35`, `25–40`, `30–45`, `35–50`, `40–55`, `45–60`, `50–65`, `60–75` | Relative organic competition, not a ranking guarantee. |
| CPC | AED ranges such as `10–25`, `20–50`, `25–75` | Directional commercial-value proxy. Actual auction CPC varies by match type, device, audience and date. |
| Priority | `P1`, `P2`, `P3`, `Hold` | P1 = near-term lead opportunity; P2 = supporting commercial/local opportunity; P3 = informational/trust; Hold = evidence required before publication. |

Before setting traffic or revenue forecasts, replace the bands for the chosen shortlist with a dated UAE export from Keyword Planner and actual query data from Search Console.
