## Budget utilization

- Expected utilization: 92%–100% of AED 1,500, or AED 1,380–1,500.
- Main underdelivery risk: limited brand volume, very narrow industrial-zone targets, low-volume exact terms, or disapproved assets.
- Main overspend misunderstanding: platform daily budgets are averages rather than strict same-day caps.
- Google states that most campaigns can spend up to twice the average daily budget on an individual day while remaining subject to a monthly limit of 30.4 times that campaign's average daily budget: [Google Ads spending limits](https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/10486637/about-spending-limits).
- Meta states that daily spend can fluctuate above the daily promotional budget while averaging within its weekly rule: [Meta budgets and schedules](https://www.facebook.com/business/ads/pricing).

If finance needs a strict AED 1,500 cash ceiling rather than platform average-budget behavior, set platform/account controls available to the account, monitor daily, and pause manually before the ceiling. Do not connect payment methods or change billing without written approval.

## Low-budget operating design

The full deliverables contain all requested ad groups and Meta ad sets, but AED 50/day is too small to run every cell at once with useful volume.

### Google

- Start with exact and phrase match only.
- Activate the two to four highest-intent ad groups per non-brand campaign; leave the remaining draft ad groups paused.
- Do not activate broad match until conversion accuracy, search-term quality, and negative coverage are proven.
- Use Search Network only initially; do not add Display expansion.
- Use presence-based location targeting.
- Run call assets only during verified hours: Monday–Saturday, 8:00 AM–6:00 PM.
- Start with controlled Manual CPC or Maximize Clicks with a management-approved max CPC after conversion tracking passes. Move to conversion bidding only after enough clean primary data accumulates.

Google notes that exact match offers the most initial control and that phrase/broad can be expanded from proven terms: [build effective keyword lists](https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/10039665). Google also advises allowing conversion cycles before judging Smart Bidding: [how bidding algorithms learn](https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/10970825).

### Meta

- Do not split AED 15/day across all planned ad sets simultaneously.
- First test one warehouse lead ad set at AED 10/day and one authority lead ad set at AED 5/day.
- Rotate construction, modification, factory/industrial, and civil/MEP concepts inside the relevant campaign.
- Keep website retargeting paused until Pixel/CAPI pass QA and the audience is large enough to deliver without excessive frequency.
- Keep lookalikes paused until a consented, high-quality seed is available.
- Use Leads objective; test Instant Form versus website lead only after tracking is reliable.
- Avoid frequent edits during the initial learning period. Meta recommends simplified structures and minimizing learning disruption: [Meta performance marketing guidance](https://www.facebook.com/business/ads/performance-marketing?locale=en_GB).

## KPIs for the first 30 days

| Layer | KPI | Initial decision use |
|---|---|---|
| Delivery | Spend and budget utilization | Detect underdelivery or accidental scope expansion |
| Search quality | Search-term relevance and negative additions | Protect budget from jobs, rental, software, residential, and informational traffic |
| Engagement | CTR and landing-page engagement | Diagnose keyword/ad/page alignment; no universal pass mark |
| Cost | CPC and raw CPL | Compare against the forecast, not as a guarantee |
| Funnel | Form-start to valid-submit rate | Detect form and tracking friction |
| Quality | Qualified-lead rate | Primary business KPI |
| Sales | Contact rate, site-inspection rate, proposal rate | Validate operational follow-up |
| Value | Pipeline value and realized revenue | Calculate ROAS only when values are verified |
| Measurement | Event discrepancy and duplicates | Must remain within the approved QA tolerance |

## Review cadence and stop-loss rules

### Daily for the first week

- Check spend, disapprovals, broken URLs, search terms, spam, duplicate events, and missed leads.
- Add only clearly irrelevant negatives.
- Do not make repeated bid/creative changes based on one day.

### Day 7

- Recommend reallocation only if a campaign cannot spend or produces clearly irrelevant traffic.
- Pause an ad group if it spends AED 150 or reaches 12–15 relevant clicks with zero meaningful engagement and the landing/tracking check is clean; review before replacing it.
- Immediately pause terms producing jobs, rentals, property sales, materials-only, or other prohibited intent.

### Day 14

- Compare keyword themes, lead validity, device, location, hour, and creative.
- Pause any source with repeated invalid leads after search-term/placement and form controls are checked.
- Do not scale a campaign from CTR alone.

### Day 30

Prepare a written performance report with:

- spend and utilization;
- impressions, clicks, CTR, CPC;
- valid leads, qualified leads, raw CPL, qualified CPL;
- search-term and placement quality;
- conversion rate by campaign and landing page;
- phone/WhatsApp/form contribution without double counting;
- pipeline value and ROAS only where verified;
- tracking discrepancies;
- recommendation to hold, reallocate, reduce, or increase.

## Scaling conditions

No increase is automatic.

Recommend a controlled increase only when:

- tracking and CRM deduplication pass;
- at least three conversion cycles or four weeks of clean data are available;
- qualified-lead definition is consistently applied;
- sales follow-up meets the approved SLA;
- qualified CPL fits management's economics;
- the campaign is budget-limited while retaining query/lead quality;
- the landing page is not the main constraint.

Use increases of about 10%–20% no more often than weekly, then observe. This is an internal control recommendation, not a platform guarantee.
